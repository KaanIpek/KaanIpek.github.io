Getting help

Something broken, a run lost, a question about the game? Write to rld.ranger07@gmail.com and say which device you are on and what happened. Replies come from the same address.

Playing co-op

  • One player opens BATTLE → CO-OP → HOST. A six-letter code appears.
  • Tap COPY to put the code on the clipboard, or INVITE to send the whole invite — the link in it opens the game straight into the join.
  • The second player taps PASTE then JOIN, and waits: the host chooses the map and starts the battle for both.
  • Co-op needs an internet connection and the same app version on both phones. Single player does not need either.

Allies (optional)

  • Tap ALLIES on the main menu. The first time, the game creates a guest player for you and shows your ally code — a name and a tag like Warden#1234. COPY it and send it to a friend; ADD theirs in the same screen. Requests are accepted or declined from the REQUESTS section.
  • When an ally is hosting a realm with a free seat, a JOIN button appears on their row; a host can also tap INVITE ALLY in the co-op card to send you a one-tap invite. Both players need the same version of Duskhold.
  • To keep your allies if you change phones, sign in: Settings → PRIVACY & ACCOUNT → Sign in with Apple (iOS) or Play Games (Android). Signing in is never required, and it does not move your campaign progress — that stays on the device.
  • Every ally row and request has BLOCK and REPORT. Reports are read within 72 hours. The full community rules are here.

Saves and resetting

Progress lives on your device. Settings → RESET PROGRESS clears it; deleting the app clears it too. Your account (if you made one) is separate: see how to delete your account — in the game under Settings → PRIVACY & ACCOUNT, or by e-mail without the app.
