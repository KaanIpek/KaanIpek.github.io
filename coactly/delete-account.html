Delete your Coactly account

Coactly · Last updated: October 2026

You can delete your Coactly account and all of its data at any time, either inside the app or by email if the app is no longer installed.

1. In the app (immediate)

  1. Open Coactly and go to the Settings tab.
  2. Tap Delete account.
  3. Confirm with Delete.

Your account and everything listed below is removed straight away and you are signed out.

2. Without the app (by email, within 30 days)

Email coactly.app@gmail.com with the subject “Delete my Coactly account”. Send it from the email address you signed in with (your Google account’s address if you used Google sign-in), so we can tell the request comes from the account’s owner. We delete the account within 30 days and reply when it is done.

What is deleted

  • Your sign-in, email address and display name.
  • Your routine items, subtasks and completions.
  • Brain-dumps, notes and planned focus sessions.
  • Mood and energy check-ins and reflections.
  • Your focus-session history.

What is not deleted, and why

  • Your subscription keeps running until you cancel it. Deleting the account does not cancel a Google Play subscription — cancel it in the Play Store under Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions.
  • Purchase records. Google Play keeps its own record of your purchases. RevenueCat, which manages subscription status for us, keeps a record tied to a random account ID (not your email or name); email us and we will delete that record too.
  • Backups. Deleted data can remain in our hosting provider’s encrypted backups until those backups expire on their normal schedule.
  • Reminders on your phone are scheduled on the device itself. Uninstalling the app removes them.

Text you sent to an AI feature is processed by Groq only to produce your result and is not retained by default. See the Privacy Policy for details.
