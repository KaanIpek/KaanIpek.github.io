Privacy Policy

Coactly · Last updated: October 2026

Coactly helps you build routines and focus alongside others. We keep data collection minimal, never sell your data, and never use it for advertising. This policy explains what we collect, who processes it for us, and the choices you have.

Health & wellbeing data: some of what you share (mood, energy, and how you feel) may be “consumer health data.” See our separate Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy.

What we collect

Your account email and display name; the routine items, tasks, and completions you create; your focus-session history; and anything you choose to add — whether you take medication (asked during set-up) and any medication reminders, mood check-ins, notes, brain-dumps, and your self-reported energy level. When you join a focus room, other participants see your first name and initials only — never your email, and never any audio or video.

How we use it

Only to run the app: to show your routine and reminders, sync focus rooms, and calculate your progress and insights. Reminders are scheduled locally on your device. We do not use your data for advertising and we do not sell it.

AI features

Some premium features use AI to help you (for example, breaking a task into steps, reframing a task you’re stuck on, planning your day, or a weekly reflection). When you tap one of these, the specific text you submit for that request — such as the task title, your brain-dump text, how you say you’re feeling, or a short summary of your activity — is sent to our AI provider, Groq, which runs Meta’s Llama model, solely to generate your result. It is processed on our behalf to produce the response; by default Groq does not retain this text after your request, and it is never used to show you ads. AI suggestions are for general wellbeing and productivity only — they are not medical advice and are always yours to edit or ignore. You can simply not use the AI features if you’d rather no text be sent.

Who processes your data

We use a small set of trusted service providers who process data on our behalf: Supabase (secure database hosting), Google Play and RevenueCat (to process and manage your subscription — we never see your card details), and Groq (to run the AI features described above). We share your data with no one else and never sell it.

Where it’s processed

Your data is stored with our backend provider (Supabase) and protected by row-level security, so only you can read or change your own rows. Our providers process data on servers in the United States. If you are in the UK, EU, or another region, your data is transferred to the US under Standard Contractual Clauses (and, for UK data, the UK Addendum) as a safeguard for that transfer.

Your rights and choices

You can view, edit, or delete any item at any time. You can permanently delete your account and all associated data from Settings → Delete account. Depending on where you live, you may also have rights under the GDPR/UK GDPR (access, correction, erasure, objection, portability) or the California CCPA/CPRA (to know, delete, correct, and opt out of “sale” or “sharing” — which we do not do). To exercise any right, email coactly.app@gmail.com and we’ll help.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit. Access to your rows is restricted to your own account by row-level security. No system is perfectly secure, so please also keep your account credentials safe.

Children

Coactly is intended for adults and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

Changes

We may update this policy as the app evolves. We’ll change the “last updated” date above, and significant changes will be highlighted in the app.

Contact

Questions? Email coactly.app@gmail.com and we’ll help.
