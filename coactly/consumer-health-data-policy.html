Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy

Coactly · Last updated: October 2026

This policy explains how Coactly handles “consumer health data” — a category defined broadly by laws such as Washington’s My Health My Data Act to include information that reflects your mental or physical wellbeing. Coactly is a general wellbeing and productivity app, not a medical or diagnostic service, but some of what you choose to share can reflect your wellbeing, so we treat it carefully. We apply this policy to everyone, not only Washington residents. It supplements our general Privacy Policy.

What consumer health data we collect

Only what you choose to enter: whether you take medication (asked during set-up) and any medication reminders you add, your mood check-ins, your self-reported energy level, and any text you write (notes, brain-dumps, or messages you submit to the AI helper) that may describe how you feel or your wellbeing. We do not collect precise location, and we never collect audio or video.

Why we collect it and how we use it

Solely to provide the app to you: to show your routine and reminders, tailor your insights, and generate the AI suggestions you ask for. We do not use consumer health data for advertising, and we do not use it to make any decision about you beyond running the features you use.

Where it comes from

Directly from you, when you enter it in the app. We do not buy or obtain it from other sources.

Who we share it with

We share consumer health data only with the service providers who help us run the app, and only so they can perform that service for us: Supabase (secure database hosting) and, when you use an AI feature, Groq (which runs the AI model to generate your result; by default it does not retain your text). We do not share consumer health data with anyone else, and we have no advertising or data-broker partners.

We do not sell your data

Coactly does not sell your consumer health data, and we will not sell it. Because we don’t sell it, we do not ask you for a separate sale authorization.

Your rights

You can ask us to confirm whether we collect, share, or sell your consumer health data and to access it; delete it; and withdraw your consent to its collection and sharing. You can delete all of it yourself at any time from Settings → Delete account, which permanently removes your account and data. If we ever deny a request, you may appeal. To exercise any right or to appeal, email coactly.app@gmail.com and we’ll respond promptly.

Consent

We collect and process your consumer health data only with your consent, which you give when you create an account and use these features, and you can withdraw it at any time by deleting the data or your account, or by emailing us.

Contact

Questions? Email coactly.app@gmail.com.
