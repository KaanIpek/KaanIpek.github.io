Shrinefall

A pixel-art action RPG for one to four players — support and contact

Getting help

Something broken, a hero lost, a question about the game? Write to rld.ranger07@gmail.com and say which device you are on and what happened. Replies come from the same address.

Playing co-op

  • One player opens CO-OP → HOST. A six-letter code appears; share it with up to three friends.
  • Each friend opens CO-OP, enters the code and taps JOIN, then picks a hero and taps READY. The host starts the world for everyone.
  • Loot is rolled per player, so nobody can take your drop; experience is shared with party members near you.
  • Co-op needs an internet connection and the same app version on every device. Playing alone needs neither.

Saves

Your heroes, levels, inventory, gold, lit shrines and settings live on your device, in the game's own save file. They are not uploaded anywhere and are not copied between devices. Deleting the app deletes them.
