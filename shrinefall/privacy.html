Shrinefall — Privacy Policy

Last updated 16 September 2026 · applies to the Shrinefall mobile game by Kaan İpek (iOS and Android), including TestFlight builds

Türkçe · Community rules · Support

Shrinefall is a pixel-art action RPG you can play alone or with up to three friends by sharing a six-letter code. There are no accounts, no advertising, no in-app purchases, no chat and no analytics kit in the game. The only time anything leaves your device is when you play co-op, and this page says exactly what that is.

Who is responsible for your data

The data controller is Kaan İpek, an individual developer based in Texas, United States, publishing as RLD Games. Contact: rld.ranger07@gmail.com. We run no servers of our own: nothing described below is stored by us.

What stays on your device

Your heroes and their levels, inventory and gold, the shrines you have lit, your settings, the player name you typed and your total play time are stored on your device by the game. They are not uploaded to us and they are not copied between devices. Deleting the app deletes them.

What leaves your device, and when

1. A guest player (Unity Authentication)

The first time you tap HOST or JOIN in the CO-OP screen, the game signs in anonymously with Unity Authentication so that Unity Relay will accept it. Unity issues a random player ID that is cached on your device so the same guest player comes back next time. No name, e-mail address or other identity is attached to it; the game never shows it and stores nothing under it.

2. Co-op sessions (Unity Relay)

Co-op traffic passes through Unity Relay, which sees the IP address it comes from for the length of the session. The host's device runs the shared world. While you are in a party, the player name you typed, the hero you chose and its level are sent to the other party members and shown in the lobby, on their screens and above your hero. This is kept only in the running game on those devices; Relay does not store your gameplay and we receive none of it.

3. Technical data seen by Unity

Like any online service, Unity Gaming Services receives your IP address, device and app identifiers and basic diagnostic data when the game talks to it. Unity acts as our processor under Unity's Game Player and App User Privacy Policy; its servers may be outside your country.

4. Apple and Google

The App Store, TestFlight and Google Play may collect crash reports and usage statistics under their own terms and your device settings, and share them with us in aggregate or anonymised form. We do not add any crash-reporting or analytics kit of our own.

How long it is kept

  • The guest player ID stays cached on your device until you delete the app. At Unity it is a random string with no data stored under it.
  • Party data (name, hero, level) lives only for the session and vanishes when the party ends.
  • Relay session data ends with the session. Unity's transient technical logs follow Unity's own retention.
  • Data on your device: until you delete the app.

Your choices

  • Play alone. Nothing leaves your device, and the game never asks you to sign in.
  • Change your player name at any time in the menu; it is only ever shown to people who have your co-op code.
  • Delete the app to remove the save file and the cached guest credential. If you want the orphaned player ID removed from Unity as well, write to us; the game does not show the ID, so tell us roughly when you last played co-op and we will look for it.

Legal basis

We process the co-op data above because you ask for that feature (performance of a contract) and the technical data in our legitimate interest in running a working, safe service. We do not sell or share personal information, and we do not use it for advertising. You may ask us at any time what we hold about you — which, outside a running co-op session, is nothing.

Children

Shrinefall's online features are intended for players aged 13 and over. The game is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has played co-op, write to us.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version appears on this page with a new date above. The Turkish and English versions say the same thing; if they differ, the English text applies outside Türkiye.
