Shrinefall — Community Rules and Terms

Last updated 16 September 2026 · Shrinefall by Kaan İpek (RLD Games)

Türkçe · Privacy policy · Support

Shrinefall is a game you can play entirely alone. The moment you host or join a co-op party, the other players can see the name you typed, and these rules apply. There is no chat, no friends list and no public matchmaking: a party only ever contains people who were given its code.

1. Player names

  • The name you type in the menu is shown to your party in the lobby, on their screens and above your hero. Keep it decent: no slurs, no sexual or hateful terms, in any language or spelling.
  • Do not use a name that impersonates another person, a real organisation or the developer.
  • Do not put anyone's personal information in a name — not yours, not anyone else's.
  • You can change your name at any time from the menu.

2. Co-op

  • A co-op code is an invitation. Share it only with people you want in your party; the host decides who to give it to.
  • Co-op sessions run on the host's device and the host's copy of the world is the one that counts. Modified clients, exploits that break a partner's run, or deliberately ruining an ally's game count as cheating.
  • Leave a party you do not enjoy: the menu takes you back to your own world at any time, with your progress intact.

3. Reporting

  • Report a player by e-mail to rld.ranger07@gmail.com with their player name, what happened and roughly when. Nothing else is needed.
  • Reports are read by a person within 72 hours of arrival. Because Shrinefall has no accounts, our tools are limited to advice and, where a rule was broken, to declining further support; hosts remain free to stop sharing their code.
  • False or spam reports are themselves a rule break.

4. The usual terms

  • Shrinefall is provided as is, for personal entertainment. We may change or withdraw the online co-op feature with reasonable notice; the single-player game keeps working offline.
  • You must be 13 or older to use the online features.
  • There is nothing to buy in Shrinefall and no advertising in it.
  • The game and its text are © 2026 Kaan İpek. Third-party art, audio and fonts keep their own licences and are credited in the game's settings screen and on the support page.
  • The laws of the State of Texas, United States, apply to these terms; consumers keep the protections of the country they live in. If a court finds one clause invalid the rest still stand.
