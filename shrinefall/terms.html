When any of this applies. Shrinefall can be finished from beginning to end without ever meeting another person, and a solo run concerns nobody but you. The moment you open a room or step into one, other players can read the name you chose, and everything below comes into force.

The name you choose

Sixteen characters, typed on the hero-choosing screen, shown to the party in the lobby and above your character while you play. Since somebody else has to read it:

  • Keep it civil. Slurs, sexual wording and hateful terms are out, in whatever language or creative spelling.
  • Do not pass yourself off as somebody else — another player, a real company, or the developer of this game.
  • Keep personal details out of it, whether they belong to you or to anyone else.
  • It is not fixed to you for ever; change it whenever you want.

A room code is an invitation

Six characters are all that stands between a stranger and your party, so treat the code as something you hand out rather than something you publish. Whoever opened the room chooses who receives it, and there is no browsing, no public list and no automatic pairing that could put an unknown player beside you.

Playing straight

  • Whoever hosts is running the world, and the host's copy of it is the one that counts.
  • Altered builds, bugs worked deliberately for advantage, and going out of your way to wreck somebody else's run all count as cheating.
  • If you are not enjoying a room, LEAVE on the co-op screen — or LEAVE TO MENU from the pause menu once you are playing — puts you back in your own world with everything you earned still on your character.

Telling us about someone

Write to rld.ranger07@gmail.com with the player's name, an account of what took place and roughly when it happened. That is the whole procedure — there is no form to fill in.

A person, not a script, opens every such message inside 72 hours of it arriving.

Be straight with us in return: inventing a complaint, or firing off the same one repeatedly, is itself a breach of these rules.

What can actually be done about it

Honesty about the limits is fairer than a promise that cannot be kept. Shrinefall has no accounts to suspend and no identity behind a player, so the realistic remedies are advice, and — where a rule has plainly been broken — declining to help that person further. The strongest tool in the game belongs to the host, who can simply stop giving out the code and start a fresh room.

Nothing is for sale

Shrinefall contains no shop, no purchasable currency, no subscription, no loot box and no advertising of any kind. The download itself is free, so no money passes between us in either direction.

The game as it is offered

  • Shrinefall is supplied as it stands, for your own entertainment, with no warranty that it will suit a particular purpose.
  • Online rooms may be altered or retired on reasonable notice. The solo game does not depend on them and will carry on working offline.
  • Thirteen is the minimum age for the online side.
  • The game, its code and its writing are © 2026 Kaan İpek. The art, audio and typefaces belong to the artists who made them, stay under their own licences, and are listed on the CREDITS screen and on the help desk.

Law, and the rest of it

These rules are read under the law of the State of Texas in the United States. That does not strip you of the consumer protections your own country grants you, which continue to apply. Should a court set aside any single paragraph here, every other paragraph stands unaffected.

One address for all of it

Reports, disagreements with a decision, and plain questions go to the same place: rld.ranger07@gmail.com.
