There is no account here to close. Shrinefall never asks for a username, a password, an e-mail address or a profile, and it has no sign-in button anywhere in it. So this page is not about shutting an account down — it is a plain inventory of the two things the game does involve, and how each one is got rid of.

The two things that exist

  • A save file on your handset Heroes, levels, bag, gold, relit shrines, the sliders in SETTINGS, the name you typed and your total playing time. It sits in the app's own storage and has never been uploaded anywhere.
  • An anonymous player identifier at Unity A random string that Unity Authentication issued the first time you opened or entered a co-op room, so that Unity Relay would accept the connection. It carries no name, no address and no history; the game does not display it and files nothing under it.

Clearing the handset: delete the app

Removing Shrinefall from the device takes the save file with it, along with the cached copy of the anonymous identifier. Nothing survives the uninstall and nothing is waiting in a cloud backup of ours to come back, because we keep no copy in the first place. That also means the erasure is final — a run deleted this way cannot be restored by us or by anybody else.

Clearing the Unity side: send one e-mail

Deleting the app leaves the identifier itself sitting unused at Unity. If you would like that removed as well, write to rld.ranger07@gmail.com and ask for it.

Because the game deliberately never shows you that string, we have to go looking for it, so please include:

  • roughly when you last opened or entered a co-op room — the month is usually enough;
  • which handset you were playing on;
  • the player name you were using at the time, if you remember it.

You will hear back at the address you wrote from once it has been dealt with, or if the details are not enough to identify anything. Ask the same way if you would rather first be told what is held: outside a live co-op room, the honest answer will be nothing.

Things this page cannot reach

Crash reports and usage figures gathered by Apple or Google belong to their platforms, not to us, and are governed by their policies and by your device settings. The record of your having downloaded the app is likewise Apple's. Neither is something we can delete on your behalf.
